About TruthLens

TruthLens is a web platform for faster, clearer news credibility analysis.

Misinformation moves quickly across digital platforms, and evaluating claims manually can be slow. TruthLens brings machine learning, natural language processing, and evidence-aware analysis together in a simple interface that helps users review suspicious content more efficiently.

Flask Web application framework
TF-IDF Text feature extraction
Classifier Prediction of fake vs real
Evidence Supportive credibility cues

What It Does

TruthLens accepts a headline, claim, or article snippet and returns a credibility-focused prediction based on learned text patterns. The goal is to make early-stage verification faster and easier for users who want a quick signal before trusting or sharing content.

Core Capabilities

Machine Learning: trained classification for real and misleading news patterns.
Natural Language Processing: transforms raw text into usable model features.
Evidence Signals: supports prediction with context-oriented credibility cues.
Web Interface: simple navigation for checking content and understanding the product.

Why It Matters

Fake news affects public opinion, decision making, and trust in information sources. TruthLens addresses this challenge by helping users identify questionable claims earlier. It is designed as a practical tool for people who want a faster first-pass credibility check on the content they read online.

Platform Scope

Input: users paste a headline, claim, or short article text.
Processing: the backend evaluates linguistic patterns and credibility indicators.
Output: the interface returns a clean prediction result for quick review.
Future Growth: more datasets, better evidence integration, and broader language support.
