Inside The Product

How TruthLens turns raw text into a credibility signal.

TruthLens is built around a simple idea: make credibility checks fast enough for everyday use while keeping the result understandable. The platform takes submitted text, converts it into model-friendly features, compares it with learned patterns, and returns a clean prediction with supporting signals.

What Happens After You Submit Text

1. Input capture
The app accepts a headline, article snippet, or short claim through the prediction form.
2. Text normalization
The content is cleaned and standardized so the model can evaluate language patterns consistently.
3. Feature conversion
TF-IDF transforms the processed text into weighted numerical features.
4. Classification
The trained model checks whether the input aligns more closely with real-news or fake-news examples.
5. Result shaping
The app presents a prediction, confidence indicator, and evidence-style signals in a user-friendly format.

What The Product Is Designed For

Fast checks: useful when users want an immediate first-pass review.
Readable output: the result is easier to understand than a raw model score.
Practical flow: submit text, review the result, and inspect supporting signals in one place.
Clear scope: the platform assists credibility review, not final fact-check verification.

Model Pipeline

Dataset: the classifier is trained on labeled real and fake news articles.
Preprocessing: tokenization, lowercasing, stopword filtering, and lemmatization help reduce noise.
Feature extraction: TF-IDF emphasizes informative words while reducing the impact of very common terms.
Classifier: Passive Aggressive learning is well suited for sparse, high-dimensional text data.

How To Read The Result

Prediction: the model's classification of the submitted content.
Confidence: a strength indicator derived from the classifier's decision margin.
Reason: a short explanation based on the strongest weighted terms found in the input.
Evidence signals: summary cues such as signal terms, processed word count, and model margin.
